# Self/Other Fusion

This technique supersedes [sahasrara]({{< relref "sahasrara" >}}) for practitioners ready to leverage [THH]({{< relref "thh" >}}) and vaporized N,N-DMT.

## The problem: subject and object

Our experience can often be divided into a subject and object. It is obvious that there are many objects to focus on, but what is less obvious is that the origin of the subject can also move. Close your eyes and touch something. Your hand can be the origin. The soles of your feet too. This is not an exhaustive list; there are others.

This subject/object, self/other divide defines individuality. To transcend individuality, we can set up two overlapping frames with opposite self/other divisions and blur them together.

## The method

Assume the [sahasrara]({{< relref "sahasrara" >}}) hand position: right (or left) hand held above the head. This posture gives you two frames with opposite self/other assignments, viewed from the same body:

- Origin at the head (customary), observing the hand as object
- Origin at the hand, observing the head as object

Neither frame is more correct than the other. Alternate between them until both are equally available, then blur them together.

### THH: locating the intersection

Take 5-30mg of THH beforehand. THH lights up a region near the two frames, the place where head-frame and hand-frame overlap.

### DMT: concentrating attention

N,N-DMT helps concentrate attention near that region, deepening the blur and making the collapse of self/other more complete. You only need enough to loosen things up, not so much that you start having visions or lose awareness of consensus reality.
