## Not ketamine

Let me be clear from the start: ketamine helps real people. Some have found relief in a clinic chair with an eye mask and a good playlist. I take those reports seriously and am glad for every one of them. If ketamine works for you, more power to you.

### The plumbing problem 🚽

Ketamine can injure the bladder. Chronic use is associated with *ketamine cystitis*: inflammation, pain, urgency, frequency, and in the worst cases a shrunken, scarred bladder. Risk appears to climb with dose and duration, so a monitored clinical course is different from a weekend habit. But "lower risk" is not "no risk." Damage is not always fully reversible.

I'm 50+ and will never try ketamine, because the last thing I need is waking up more often at night to pee. I diligently eat cranberries with dinner. Sleep is a precious resource. I guard it like a dragon guarding a hoard.

Ketamine is a fine tool, for other people.
