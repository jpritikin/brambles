INWARD PERSPECTIVE EXPLORER - PRIMER
======================================

What this is
-------------
An animated widget for the "Inward perspective" axis of the Psychological
Characterization scale (see data/psychScale.json and psych-scale.ts).
Embedded via <div id="inward-perspective-explorer"> in
content/docs/psychoactive/_index.md. Three vertices - Self (1st), blended
(2nd), unblended (3rd) - form a triangle. A dashed, wobbling "attention
perimeter" (visual language borrowed from urbb-web's StarBorder) wraps
around a Self circle (radius grows with ambient "Self energy", set by hand
via a rotary knob at the Self vertex) and one fixed-radius circle per
drifting emoji "part" that fades in and out on its own, plus dose sliders
for named substances. At high enough drug-driven "blending pressure", the
perimeter can instead collapse onto a single part (or a small linked group),
excluding Self entirely - see "Private reverie" below.

Entry point: src/inward-perspective-explorer.ts's `InwardPerspectiveExplorer`
class, instantiated directly against the container div at module load (no
separate init() - see the bottom of the file).

Core architecture
------------------

1. src/inward/geometry.ts - triangle/vertex constants and pure math, no
   drug/UI knowledge.
   - `VERTICES`/`VERTEX_BY_NAME`: the three triangle corners (Self, blended,
     unblended) with fixed screen positions.
   - `clampPartPosition`: keeps a part inside the triangle's full extent
     (Self down to the blended/unblended corners) - staying out of the area
     near Self is instead handled by the Self-proximity gravity force in
     inward-perspective-explorer.ts's tick loop. Both axes are clamped
     together in one function because at a given height the triangle's
     slanted left/right edges are narrower than the full corner-to-corner
     width - clamping x and y independently would let points escape near
     the slanted edges.
   - `sigmoid(x, midpoint, steepness)`: generic logistic curve, used
     throughout for smooth 0..1 thresholds (e.g. the high-Self-energy
     spawn/reap cutoff, the Private reverie focus-lock trigger) instead of
     hard `>=` comparisons that would snap abruptly.

2. src/inward/drugData.ts - the substance model, no rendering/UI code.
   - `DrugEffect`: one substance's dosing shape (`doseSteps` for a few
     discrete doses vs. a free 0-100 slider), a `pull` on the triangle's
     three vertices (dormant - see "Known WIP" below, not currently
     consumed anywhere), its Self-energy contribution
     (`selfEnergyBoostSteps`/`selfEnergyBoostMax`), and optional direct
     per-part pushes that bypass ambient Self energy entirely:
     `unblendPushMax` (push every part away from blended - THH's
     mechanism) and `blendPushMax` (push every part toward blended - N,N-
     DMT's mechanism, the blended-side mirror). Both are on the shared 0..1
     "blending force" scale and may exceed 1. `rendersAsPart` (cannabis:
     instead spawns its own drifting emoji part near a vertex).
   - `doseUnitRange` (linear, e.g. cannabis 4-12mg, N,N-DMT 1-60mg) vs.
     `doseUnitLogRange` (exponential, e.g. THH 1-1000mg - most slider
     travel covers the low end) control how a free slider's 0-100%
     fraction is displayed.
   - `DRUGS`/`DRUG_BY_KEY`: the substance list, each with real-world dosing
     notes in comments (5-MAPB is binary 80mg/100mg with no inactive step;
     5-MeO-DMT is threshold-or-breakthrough; THH's unblending bypasses
     Self energy entirely and can reach 0.9 on the normalized blend-force
     scale; N,N-DMT gives a modest Self-energy lift (selfEnergyBoostMax
     0.15) but a strong direct blendPushMax (0.9) - past a threshold this
     is what triggers Private reverie).
   - `INTERACTIONS`: explicit named pairs only, severity "contraindicated"
     (hard-blocked, e.g. 5-MAPB + 5-MeO-DMT serotonin syndrome risk) or
     "caution" (warned, not blocked). A pair absent from this table (e.g.
     DMT + THH, the Daime brew combination) gets no warning at all - no
     substance is assumed risky with "anything else." THH's unblendPushMax
     and N,N-DMT's blendPushMax are opposing forces that net-cancel before
     driving Private reverie (see InwardPerspectiveExplorer.updateFocusLock
     below) - enough THH can prevent DMT alone from reaching hyper-focus,
     and there's deliberately no INTERACTIONS warning for this; it's normal
     force cancellation, not a safety concern.

3. src/inward/part.ts - `Part` type: a drifting emoji's live state (x/y/vx/
   vy, opacity, fadingOut, its SVG element, and `blendPropensity` - see
   below). The emoji is rendered directly at (x, y) - no separate
   rendered-position indirection (attnPerimeter.ts's EMOJI_VERTICAL_CENTER_
   OFFSET compensates for the baseline-vs-visual-center gap when building
   circles/capsules from a part's position - see below). `extraLinkTo` is a
   possibly-null reference to another Part, rolled once at spawn time (see
   attnPerimeter.ts below); it's stored directionally but represents an
   undirected connector - both InwardPerspectiveExplorer.linkedGroup (Private
   reverie's transitive group-finding) and attnPerimeter.ts's capsule
   builders treat "linked to" as symmetric. The perimeter-removal code in
   inward-perspective-explorer.ts nulls out any other part's `extraLinkTo`
   that pointed at a part being removed, so a stale link can't keep the
   perimeter enclosing empty space after that part is gone. `hyperFocused`
   is true while a part is Private reverie's target (or in its linked
   group, cannabis excepted - see below); exempts it from spontaneous
   reaping. Plus `PARTS_PALETTE`, the pool random parts are drawn from.

4. src/inward/attnPerimeter.ts - pure attention-perimeter geometry,
   consuming only `Circle[]`/`Capsule[]`/`Part[]` (never drug data
   directly - drug effects into shrink-wrap are partially wired, see
   below).
   - `EMOJI_VERTICAL_CENTER_OFFSET`: part emoji are SVG <text> with y as
     the baseline, not the glyph's visual center; every circle/capsule
     endpoint built from a part's (x, y) subtracts this so it centers on
     the glyph instead of sitting below it. Single source of truth,
     imported by inward-perspective-explorer.ts for its own overlays
     (conflict ring, popup anchor) rather than duplicated.
   - The perimeter is not a single circle at a computed centroid+radius; it's
     a metaball isoline wrapped around one circle per "thing" (Self, each
     part), extracted via marching squares:
   - `buildCircles(selfEnergy, parts, focusedParts?)`: one circle for Self,
     centered on the Self vertex, with `selfCircleRadius(selfEnergy)`
     growing from a min up to 2x that at full Self energy; one fixed-radius
     circle per part (`PART_CIRCLE_RADIUS`, half the emoji's rendered
     font-size), scaled by the part's opacity so a fading-in/out part's
     circle ramps smoothly instead of popping to full size. When
     `focusedParts` is non-empty (Private reverie active), Self is excluded
     entirely and only those parts' circles are returned - so circles[0]
     is not guaranteed to be Self; callers needing the Self circle
     specifically must check for focus mode first.
   - `buildConnectorCapsules(selfCircle, parts, shrinkWrap)`: every part is
     explicitly linked to Self (a star topology, so the perimeter can never
     lose connectivity to a part however far it drifts), plus each part
     optionally carries one extra link to another part - `Part.extraLinkTo`,
     rolled once at spawn time with even odds when other parts already
     exist (`InwardPerspectiveExplorer.rollExtraLink`), fixed for that
     part's lifetime rather than re-rolled per frame. Each link becomes a
     `Capsule` (a line segment with a radius); `connectorRadiusFor
     (shrinkWrap)` sets that radius the same direction as the isoline
     threshold - loose shrink-wrap gives a fat corridor, tight narrows it
     toward a thin thread.
   - `buildFocusCapsules(focusedParts, shrinkWrap)`: Private reverie's
     capsule builder - no Self hub to fan out from, so it connects focused
     parts along their actual extraLinkTo edges (undirected) instead of a
     star from the first part, since the group can be a transitive chain
     (A links to B, B links to C) where A and C aren't directly linked.
   - `fieldAt(x, y, circles, capsules)`: the metaball scalar field - each
     circle and each capsule contributes an inverse-square falloff term
     (1 at its own edge, capsules measured from the nearest point on their
     segment), summed with a small strictly-positive `BACKGROUND_FIELD` so
     the isoline can never split into disconnected components even where
     no capsule reaches.
   - `thresholdFor(shrinkWrap)`: the isoline value the perimeter is drawn
     at. Low (loose, shrinkWrap=0) sits far outside every circle's true
     edge, giving one big soft padded oval; high (tight, shrinkWrap=1)
     hugs the circles closely but is capped at 0.78 rather than
     approaching 1 - at 1 the isoline would sit almost exactly at a part
     circle's nominal radius, which is smaller than the emoji glyph's
     visual size, making the dashed ring read as clipping through the
     emoji instead of wrapping outside it. 0.78 keeps ~15% radius margin
     at the tightest setting.
   - `marchingSquaresContour`: sizes its sampling grid off both the overall
     bounding-box span AND the thinnest capsule radius present (not span
     alone) - a thin connector (tight shrinkWrap, CONNECTOR_RADIUS_TIGHT=3)
     between two widely-spaced circles is the narrowest thing the grid
     ever needs to resolve; sizing cells only off span could make that
     corridor thinner than one cell, causing the marching-squares/greedy-
     chaining step to mis-stitch two circles' isolines into one malformed
     loop instead of a proper closed contour wrapping both (this was a
     real bug, most visible in Private reverie's sparse 2-circle case).
     The bounding-box margin itself is also derived (not fixed) from the
     actual threshold and largest circle radius, so a loose (low-
     threshold) isoline - which sits much farther from its circle than a
     tight one - doesn't get clipped by too-small a margin.
   - `erodeContour` (called from `buildAttnPerimeterPath` after resampling,
     before the wobble pass): at tight shrinkWrap, three-plus mutually-
     nearby circles' overlapping fields still sum above threshold across
     their whole convex hull's interior (e.g. the puffed-out middle of a
     Self+2-parts triangle) even though the isoline hugs each circle's own
     edge closely elsewhere - summing fields doesn't erode a shape the way
     intersecting/subtracting them would. Post-processes each already-
     extracted contour point by pulling it inward toward its nearest
     feature (circle edge or capsule edge) by up to that point's "slack" -
     how far past the nearest feature's true edge it currently sits -
     scaled by erosion strength (0 below `EROSION_START_SHRINK_WRAP` (0.5),
     ramping to full over the rest of the range, so a loose boundary still
     reads as one soft padded oval rather than pinching in early). A point
     already on some feature's edge has ~0 slack and doesn't move, so
     erosion can never pull a point past a feature it's supposed to still
     enclose - only the slack-everywhere middle erodes, keeping every
     circle/capsule connected.
   - `buildAttnPerimeterPath(circles, parts, shrinkWrap, wobblePhase,
     focusedParts?)`: samples the field on the uniform grid, extracts the
     isoline via marching squares, greedily chains the resulting segments
     into one closed polygon, resamples it to N evenly-spaced-by-arclength
     points, erodes it (see above), applies the sine wobble (perturbing
     each point outward along the vector from the circles' centroid), and
     renders it as the same Catmull-Rom-style dashed SVG path as before.
   - `computeTargetShrinkWrap(selfEnergy, blendPressure)`: baseline is
     `0.3 * (1 - selfEnergy)` - a depleted Self lets the boundary go
     loose/soft, a maxed-out Self has nothing left to widen it. N,N-DMT's
     blendPressure (see InwardPerspectiveExplorer.blendPressure) then adds
     `0.5 * blendPressure` on top, clamped to 1 - the same pressure that
     drives Private reverie's focus lock also tightens the ordinary
     (non-focus-locked) perimeter.
   - `BLEND_FORCE_SCALE`: converts the normalized 0..1 "blending force"
     scale (shared by ambient Self-energy push, blendPropensity,
     unblendPushMax, blendPushMax) into actual per-frame velocity units.
     Purely a presentation/physics-scale constant - psychological
     measurements (Private reverie's focus-lock sigmoid, the force
     popup's displayed magnitudes) deliberately read the pre-scale 0..1
     inputs instead, not values multiplied by this.

5. src/inward/selfEnergyKnob.ts - `SelfEnergyKnob`: the rotary control at
   the Self vertex, fully self-contained (owns its own SVG ring/wedge/
   label DOM and pointer-drag handling).
   - Ambient Self energy = a drug-set baseline floor (e.g. 5-MAPB) plus the
     user's knob adjustment on top of it, via `energy` getter. Low Self
     energy shrinks the Self circle toward `SELF_CIRCLE_MIN_RADIUS`
     (attnPerimeter.ts), so the perimeter reads as small/collapsed even
     with no parts present - it no longer drifts toward blended the way
     the prior centroid-based model did.
   - The knob's `userFraction` is a 0..1 fraction *of the range above the
     current baseline* - not an absolute value. With no drug baseline the
     range is [0, 0.5]; with 5-MAPB's baseline at 0.25, the range becomes
     [0.25, 0.75]. `setBaseline()` reinterprets (never resets) this
     fraction when the baseline changes, so administering/cancelling a
     drug shifts the knob's *range*, not silently its position.
   - Drag math tracks *relative* rotation (delta from the previous raw
     atan2 angle, unwrapped and accumulated) rather than an absolute angle,
     specifically to avoid a jump when the cursor crosses the wrap point
     directly below the knob (atan2 discontinuity at +-180deg). A delta can
     never exceed 180deg by construction, and clamping only trims from
     whichever end the travel is already approaching - so the rendered
     angle always passes through every intermediate value.
   - Standard 270deg sweep: 0% at bottom-left (7:30), straight up = 50%
     (12 o'clock), 100% at bottom-right (4:30), with a dead zone across
     the bottom.
   - The caller (InwardPerspectiveExplorer) reads `.energy` live every
     animation frame - there's no change callback, since the knob's state
     is polled rather than pushed.

6. src/inward/doseController.ts - `DoseController`: owns dosing state
   (which drugs are administered, at what dose) and the picker UI wired to
   it - the dose slider, Administer button, contraindication blocking, the
   warning banner, and the applied-substances list rendered in the SVG.
   - Dosing is staged: moving the slider only updates `pendingDose` and its
     label; nothing takes effect (cannabis part, Self-energy baseline)
     until Administer is clicked. `doses[key]` only updates on administer.
     unblendPushMax/blendPushMax are read live from `doses[key]` every
     frame in inward-perspective-explorer.ts's tick loop, so they track
     administered dose directly.
   - `administeredKeys` tracks activity independently of dose value -
     needed because cannabis's 0 fraction is a real 4mg dose (its
     doseUnitRange floor), not "inactive." Cancelling (the applied list's
     X) removes the key and zeroes the dose.
   - 5-MAPB is exclusive in both directions: administering it while
     anything else is active is blocked, and administering anything else
     while it's active is blocked (`mapbExclusivityError`).
   - Constructed with an `onDoseChange` callback so the parent
     (InwardPerspectiveExplorer) can react to the parts of a dose change
     that live outside dosing itself: syncing the cannabis part and
     recomputing the Self-energy baseline.

7. src/inward/readoutText.ts - word banks for the "Subjective read-out"
   text: `focusPhrase(circleCount, shrinkWrap, selfEnergy)` (keyed off a
   score combining how many circles the perimeter currently encloses -
   more = more diffuse - and how tightly it's shrink-wrapped around them -
   tighter = more focused - via `sqrt(circleCount) - shrinkWrap`, not a
   geometric radius estimate, since there's no single radius once the
   perimeter wraps a variable number of circles; if that score lands in the
   narrowest "hyper-focused" bucket AND Self energy is at or below the
   depleted threshold (0.2), returns "sleep" instead - a hyper-focused
   reading at near-zero Self energy isn't concentration, it's the
   perimeter collapsing for lack of any ambient energy to hold it open).
   The caller (InwardPerspectiveExplorer.updateReadout) short-circuits
   past focusPhrase entirely to show "Private reverie" instead whenever
   focus-lock is active, so "sleep" and "Private reverie" are mutually
   exclusive, distinct states. Also `selfQualitiesFor` (keyed off Self
   energy), `blendIntensityWord` (keyed off a part's blendPropensity).

8. src/inward/drugWheel.ts - `DrugWheel`: the pie-menu substance picker UI
   (collapsed button -> radial slice menu), independent of dosing logic.

9. src/inward-perspective-explorer.ts - the top-level class. Owns:
   - The SVG scaffold and injected CSS (constructor). Also builds the
     debug panel's shrinkWrap-override checkbox+slider (`?debug=1`,
     `debugShrinkWrapOverride`) for manually pinning the perimeter's
     tightness regardless of computeTargetShrinkWrap's normal drivers, and
     the manual-part panel's Sweep button (`sweepOldestPart`) which removes
     the single least-recently-added part (skipping the cannabis part,
     whose lifecycle is dose-driven) - a deliberate one-shot user action,
     unlike automatic reaping, so it's allowed to remove a hyperFocused
     part if the user chooses to.
   - Spontaneous part spawning/reaping: a new part every 10-20s
     (`scheduleSpawn`, probability 1/n where n = current spontaneous part
     count, so it self-limits), stochastically reaped back down every
     fixed 1s once more than one is present (`scheduleReap`, 10% chance per
     tick above the floor). The high-Self-energy spawn/reap cutoff is a
     sigmoid (`highSelfEnergyOdds`, centered at `HIGH_SELF_ENERGY_CENTER`
     0.9, steepness 20) rather than a hard `>=` threshold - its output is
     used as a per-check *probability* (spawn suppressed with that
     probability; reap forced with that probability, bypassing the normal
     "keep >=1 part" floor and 10% chance), so the transition is gradual
     rather than snapping abruptly at a fixed cutoff. `hyperFocused` parts
     (Private reverie's target/group, cannabis excepted) are exempt from
     this reaping - they're currently the entire object of attention.
     `spontaneousParts` (oldest-first) excludes the cannabis part, which
     tracks its own dose instead of being reaped.
   - `rollExtraLink()`: called at every part-spawn site (spontaneous,
     manual, cannabis). 50% odds of picking one existing part at random to
     be this new part's `extraLinkTo`, giving the attention perimeter an
     occasional part-to-part connector on top of the always-present
     part-to-Self one built in attnPerimeter.ts.
   - "Private reverie" (`updateFocusLock`, called once a second alongside
     reap): while net blending pressure is high enough, the attention
     perimeter can collapse onto a single part (plus its linked group,
     found transitively - see below), excluding Self entirely.
     - `blendPressure()`/`unblendPressure()`: sum `blendPushMax`/
       `unblendPushMax * dose` across all active drugs (generalizes to any
       drug with either field set, not hardcoded to specific substances).
       Net pressure driving focus-lock is
       `max(0, blendPressure() - unblendPressure())` - opposing drugs
       cancel before the sigmoid, so enough THH (unblendPressure) can
       prevent N,N-DMT's blendPressure alone from reaching hyper-focus.
     - The sigmoid (center 0.7, steepness 20) is checked against
       hysteretic thresholds: `FOCUS_LOCK_ENTER_ODDS` (0.8) to engage,
       `FOCUS_LOCK_EXIT_ODDS` (0.3, lower) to release - entering takes a
       decisive push, exiting takes a real drop, so it doesn't flicker
       in/out right at one threshold.
     - On entry: the highest-blendPropensity non-fading, non-cannabis part
       becomes the target; `linkedGroup(target)` finds every part
       transitively reachable via `extraLinkTo` treated as an *undirected*
       edge (checked both directions - "does X link to Y" and "does Y link
       to X" - and followed through chains, e.g. A-B-C all group together
       if A links to B and B links to C), since the stored field is
       directional but the connector it produces isn't. Cannabis, if
       present and not fading, always joins the focus group too (it stays
       part of the attention perimeter regardless of reverie) but is never
       marked `hyperFocused` itself, since its lifecycle stays dose-driven
       rather than focus-lock-driven. Every other part not in the group
       fades out immediately - Private reverie is about just the focused
       parts, so nothing else should linger unseen outside the now
       Self-excluding perimeter.
     - On exit (net pressure sigmoid drops below the exit threshold):
       `hyperFocused` is cleared on every focused part and the group is
       emptied; cannabis (never having been marked hyperFocused) is
       unaffected structurally, but no longer forced into the circle set
       either.
     - If a focused part is removed by some other path (Sweep, cannabis
       cancel) while reverie is active, the whole group drops (see the
       tick loop's removal cleanup) so focus-lock re-evaluates cleanly
       next check instead of referencing a gone part.
   - Part dragging (`startDrag`) and the cannabis part's lifecycle
     (`syncCannabisPart` - spawns/despawns it near the blended vertex,
     keeps its blendPropensity locked to the THC dose fraction rather than
     randomly sampled like other parts).
   - The animation tick loop (`tick`): per-frame part physics - random
     wander (as occasional impulses, not continuous jitter: a ~2.5s-average
     Poisson-ish kick chance per frame via `WANDER_KICK_CHANCE_PER_SEC`,
     each kick a single discrete nudge of fixed `WANDER_KICK_STRENGTH` in a
     random direction, not scaled by dt since it's an event not a rate -
     this reads as "occasional larger impulses" rather than the
     continuously-nervous look of per-frame Gaussian-ish noise), push away
     from blended scaled by ambient Self energy *linearly* (not through a
     sigmoid - Self energy itself already ramps smoothly via the knob), a
     separate linear "unblending" push along the Self-to-part line
     (unblendPush, from THH-like drugs) plus the mirrored blendPush (from
     N,N-DMT-like drugs) added onto each part's own blendPropensity, mutual
     part repulsion - updates each part's (x, y), which the emoji is
     rendered at directly (no separate rendered-position indirection);
     then calls into attnPerimeter.ts (`buildCircles`/
     `computeTargetShrinkWrap`/`buildAttnPerimeterPath`, all now taking
     `this.focusedParts` where relevant) to rebuild and re-render the
     wrapping perimeter. `shrinkWrap` itself is still smoothed frame-to-
     frame via lerp toward its target, same as before.
   - Every named force is recorded per part even at zero magnitude (stable
     popup rows frame to frame), each carrying a `displayMag` - the
     pre-BLEND_FORCE_SCALE 0..1 value for forces that have one
     (Self-energy unblend = selfPushFactor + unblendPush; Blend urgency =
     blendPropensity + blendPush) - so the force-breakdown popup's legend
     shows true psychological-scale numbers, not raw velocity units mixed
     with BLEND_FORCE_SCALE. Self differentiation (the inverse-square
     "gravity" pulling a part down away from Self, renamed from "Self
     proximity") and Part repulsion have no such scale and fall back to
     raw magnitude (Part repulsion is additionally omitted from the
     legend entirely - purely a display/anti-overlap mechanic with no
     psychological meaning - though it still participates in the vector
     plot and computeConflict below, since it does affect actual motion).
   - `computeConflict(forces)`: reads only "Self-energy unblend" and
     "Blend urgency"'s `displayMag` (the only two forces sharing the 0..1
     blending-force scale, and always exactly opposed along the fixed
     blended<->unblended axis) and returns `min(a, b)` - deliberately
     un-normalized (not a sum-of-magnitudes ratio), so two strongly
     opposed forces (0.8 vs 0.9) read as more conflicted than two weakly
     opposed ones (0.1 vs 0.2) rather than both reading as "fully
     conflicted." Drives the per-part conflict ring's opacity, which fades
     in starting at conflict 0.75 and reaches full opacity at 1.0.
   - Part removal (once a fading-out part's opacity drops below 0.02) also
     walks the remaining parts and nulls any `extraLinkTo` that pointed at
     the part being removed, so the perimeter's connector capsules can't
     keep enclosing a part's last position after it's gone; and drops the
     whole Private reverie group if a focused part is the one removed.
   - The debug overlay (`?debug=1` in the URL): draws each Self/part
     circle and connector capsule directly (`ipe-debug-circle`/
     `ipe-debug-capsule`), plus a coarse sampled-field grid
     (`ipe-debug-field-dot`, red = inside the current isoline threshold,
     gray = outside) so the metaball field marching squares walks is
     visible directly rather than only its end result (the perimeter
     path). The text panel reports selfEnergy, circle count (with a
     Private-reverie note when active), shrinkWrap (current + target), the
     isoline threshold, and blendPressure/unblendPressure. Also renders a
     shrinkWrap-override checkbox+slider (debug-only) for pinning the
     perimeter's tightness manually.
   - Wires SelfEnergyKnob and DoseController together: DoseController's
     `onDoseChange` callback triggers `syncCannabisPart` and
     `selfEnergyKnob.setBaseline(doseController.computeSelfEnergyBaseline())`.

Design notes worth knowing before touching the physics
--------------------------------------------------------
- Everything pushing a part or the attention perimeter is normalized to a
  shared 0..1 "blending force" scale (ambient Self-energy push, a part's own
  blendPropensity, THH's unblendPushMax and N,N-DMT's blendPushMax - both
  allowed to exceed 1). `BLEND_FORCE_SCALE` (attnPerimeter.ts) is the only
  place that scale gets converted into actual velocity units, so tuning
  relative strengths means changing the 0..1 inputs, not per-force
  velocity constants scattered around. Psychological measurements (the
  force popup's displayed magnitudes via `PartForce.displayMag`, Private
  reverie's focus-lock sigmoid) deliberately read the pre-scale values,
  never anything multiplied by BLEND_FORCE_SCALE - it's a presentation/
  physics constant, not part of the psychological model.
- The perimeter is a metaball isoline, not a parametric shape - there is no
  single "radius" or "centroid" to read or animate directly anymore.
  Anything that used to reach for those (readout text, debug overlay) now
  either takes the underlying `Circle[]`/`shrinkWrap` inputs, or a derived
  scalar like circle count. Don't reintroduce a cached centroid/radius
  pair - it was the prior model's core assumption and the isoline+marching-
  squares approach doesn't preserve it.
- Connectivity (Self and every part always joined by one connected
  perimeter, at any distance) is guaranteed structurally via explicit
  `Capsule` corridors (`buildConnectorCapsules`/`buildFocusCapsules`), not
  by tuning the metaball field's falloff or background bias. Don't try to
  fix a disconnected-perimeter bug by raising `BACKGROUND_FIELD` - that
  keeps the isoline technically connected but the connecting neck goes
  arbitrarily thin/invisible at long range. Also don't assume the grid
  resolves any corridor for free: `marchingSquaresContour`'s cell size is
  derived from the thinnest capsule radius present specifically because a
  fat-circles/thin-corridor shape (e.g. Private reverie's sparse 2-circle
  case) can otherwise slip through undetected and produce a malformed
  chained contour instead of a proper closed loop.
- Self energy is never a single stored number - it's always derived
  (`SelfEnergyKnob.energy` = baseline + fraction*range). If you need the
  live value elsewhere, read the getter every frame; don't cache it.
- Applied-list X-position uses a rough per-character width estimate
  instead of `getBBox()`, deliberately, to avoid forcing a synchronous
  layout reflow on every dose-list update.
- Part emoji baseline-vs-visual-center: `EMOJI_VERTICAL_CENTER_OFFSET`
  lives in attnPerimeter.ts as the single source of truth (imported, not
  duplicated, by inward-perspective-explorer.ts for its own overlays).
  Any new code building a Circle or Capsule endpoint from a part's raw
  (x, y) needs to subtract this, or the perimeter will visibly sit below
  the emoji it's meant to wrap - this was a real, easy-to-miss bug.
