Most cannabis formulations, built around just THC and CBD, can work for intimacy—but they require a certain amount of mental effort to stay in a good headspace. That's the kind of effort you don't need to be making.

### Relax Blend

Adding a third cannabinoid—CBN (cannabinol)—to the THC+CBD combination creates something noticeably more stable. In a 1:1:1 ratio of Δ9-THC, CBD, and CBN, the experience is self-regulating. You don't need to steer your mood. The blend handles that, freeing your attention for your partner, your body, and the moment.

A Δ8 version works nearly as well. Because Δ8-THC is somewhat milder than Δ9, the ratio shifts to 4:1:1 (Δ8-THC : CBD : CBN) to achieve a comparable effect. The subjective experience is largely the same.

Both versions are straightforward DIY projects. The ratio that matters most is CBD:CBN—keep those equal and adjust THC to your preference.

### Why This Helps with Sex Specifically

The relax blend supports intimacy in four distinct ways:

**Mild cognitive narrowing is presence.** Mild short-term memory impairment makes it difficult to sustain the long chains of thought that pull you out of the moment—rumination, self-monitoring, rehearsal. What remains is sensation and the person in front of you.

**It slows things down.** Orgasm is an autonomic sequence of contractions. Cannabis appears to alter the perception of that sequence—stretching what is usually a few moments into a more expansive and satisfying experience.

**It gets out of its own way.** The stability of this blend means the cannabis itself isn't competing for your attention.

**It softens the afterglow's edge.** For hours after orgasm, empathy runs higher and emotional defenses run lower—a tender window, but also one where stress lands harder than usual. The relax blend's stability carries into this period, buffering that vulnerability.

### When the Blend Isn't Enough

The relax blend addresses the physiological side of intimacy. But chemistry only goes so far. If you find that this relax blend doesn't adequately dissolve self-consciousness during sex, that's useful information. It points to internal obstacles. Some psychological patterns may have taken on a life of their own—habitual self-criticism, anxiety about performance, or old shame that surfaces at the worst moments. These are the kinds of internal dynamics that [Internal Family Systems therapy](https://estrelacounseling.com/) can help with.

### Practical Notes

Too much of this blend will put you to sleep—that's the main risk to calibrate around. Allow 60–90 minutes after oral administration before drawing conclusions about the dose.

[Cannabis]({{< relref "cannabis" >}}) is a good partner for intimacy when it doesn't demand attention. The relax blend is designed not to.
