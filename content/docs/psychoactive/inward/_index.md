### Inward Perspective Explorer

Drag the Self energy knob, administer doses of the modeled substances, and drag parts around to see how the attention perimeter responds.

An interactive widget for the "Inward perspective" axis 1️⃣2️⃣3️⃣ of the [Psychological Characterization]({{< relref "../#psychological-characterization" >}}) scale: 1️⃣ = Self, calm at the center. 2️⃣ = blended, a part takes over and its feelings become your reality. 3️⃣ = unblended, you relate to feelings with curiosity and distance, as a part you're with rather than a part you are.
